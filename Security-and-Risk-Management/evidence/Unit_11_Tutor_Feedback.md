# Unit 11 Individual Project – Tutor Feedback

**Assessment:** Executive Summary  
**Overall grade:** 75% (provisional)

## Knowledge and Understanding – 23/30

The submission clearly distinguished hazard occurrence from escalation into material harm and supported the analysis with a detailed spreadsheet model. The recovery discussion considered detection time, replication lag, stock consistency and complete customer transactions. The proposed regional configuration required greater specificity, and successful testing was still needed before claiming that the architecture met the recovery requirements.

## Criticality – 23/30

The shared stress factor, sensitivity analysis and conditional pilot recommendation demonstrated thoughtful evaluation. The submission recognised that residual-risk acceptance requires management judgement and linked expansion to measurable evidence. The assumed reductions in escalation rates needed stronger justification. Alternative control-effectiveness and dependency scenarios would have shown whether the investment recommendation remained reliable under less favourable assumptions.

## Use of Relevant Sources – 15/20

Academic literature and official technical guidance supported the modelling, recovery and governance discussion. Provider documentation was used effectively to identify possible failure-detection delays. The numerical ranges remained largely judgement-based, however, and stronger direct evidence and published research on vendor lock-in were required.

## Structure and Presentation – 7/10

The summary opened with a clear decision, presented useful input tables and concluded with ordered implementation requirements. The workbook separated assumptions, simulation and outputs logically. The written summary could have been more concise, while sensitivity and convergence results should have updated from the simulation rather than remaining fixed.

## Academic Integrity – 7/10

The submission disclosed scenario-based inputs and supplied formulas, assumptions and calculations. However, the workbook described a fixed seed while using unseeded `RANDARRAY`, so exact reproducibility was not established. The report and workbook also contained slightly different results, and bibliographic details required consistent checking.

## Actions Carried Forward

- Evidence control-effectiveness estimates and compare adverse scenarios.
- Represent important dependencies rather than relying on one shared factor.
- Test the complete recovery service before claiming RTO/RPO compliance.
- Make all supporting workbook outputs dynamic or freeze and label a verified version.
- Reconcile every reported number and verify every reference before submission.
